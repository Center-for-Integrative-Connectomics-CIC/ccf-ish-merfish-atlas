Datasets

Two atlases, six data sources, one space

Each dataset is read from its original publisher and kept as is: ISH volumes stream from the Blue Brain / EBRAINS bucket; MERFISH cells, cell types and coordinates come from the Allen Brain Cell Atlas. Each of the five MERFISH brains is shown on its own — pick one, or compare all five region by region.

How it is built

From the original files to one CCF

1 · Reference spaceCCFv3BBP (Piluso et al.) at 25 µm: annotation, template and 887 region surfaces.
2 · AlignmentMERFISH cells carry Allen CCFv3 coordinates; CCFv3BBP = CCFv3 + 14 voxels AP, verified whole-brain against the CCF-2020 annotation the cells used.
3 · Cells & typesEvery cell at 1 µm, with its WMB cell type (34 classes → 5,322 clusters), its own CCF label and the CCFv3BBP label at its position.
4 · GenesISH volumes as published (25 µm); MERFISH log2 expression per cell; region means for both, and their concordance per gene and brain.
