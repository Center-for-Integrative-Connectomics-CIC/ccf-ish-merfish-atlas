1 · One space

MERFISH (Allen CCFv3) onto ISH (CCFv3BBP)

The MERFISH cells come with Allen CCFv3 coordinates; the ISH volumes live on CCFv3BBP, which extends CCFv3 anteriorly and subdivides some regions. The CCF-2020 annotation the cells were registered with was placed on the CCFv3BBP grid under integer shifts and compared voxel by voxel. The best shift is used for every cell.

2 · Cells

Where each brain's cells land

Each cell keeps two region labels: its own Allen CCF-2020 parcellation (the data owners' registration) and the CCFv3BBP structure at its position after the shift. Their agreement measures how much region assignments depend on the atlas; the viewer lets you switch between them. Exact-label disagreement is dominated by cells near region borders and by CCFv3BBP's finer subdivisions.

3 · Genes

Do ISH and MERFISH put a gene in the same regions?

For every gene measured by both, per brain, across regions at ontology depth 5–8 with ≥ 50 cells and ≥ 500 voxels, using ISH region means and MERFISH region means (own CCF labels): Spearman ρ (whole ranking), Pearson r (driven by where the gene is high, so it suits sparse markers whose many near-zero regions make ranks noisy) and peak agreement (the percentile, in each atlas, of the other atlas' top region; 1.00 = same top region). ISH values (voxel intensities) and MERFISH values (mean log2 per cell) have different units, so only these scale-free measures are compared. The MERFISH-vs-MERFISH correlation between two animals measured with the same method is the practical ceiling.

4 · Data handling

What is changed, and what is not

  • ISH volumes are the published 25 µm files, streamed unchanged from EBRAINS; the viewer only reorients them (NIfTI LR×AP×DV → ASR, checked against the Python decoder voxel by voxel).
  • Cell positions are the Allen Brain Cell Atlas CCFv3 coordinates, shifted by 14 × 25 µm along AP and stored at 1 µm.
  • MERFISH expression is the ABC "log2" matrix, quantised per gene to 8 bits (value = q/255 × gene max; zero stays zero). The round-trip error is at most one step (≈0.4% of the gene's maximum); detection is exact.
  • Region statistics are computed from the full-precision data (float64 sums) and stored as float16.
  • Cell types are the ABC atlas WMB taxonomy labels per cell (34 classes, 338 subclasses, 1,201 supertypes, 5,322 clusters), with each cell's mapping-quality score.
5 · Limits

Read the data with these in mind

  • Each MERFISH brain was registered to the CCF by its data owners, section by section; that registration has its own error, which this site does not correct. Thin layers and small nuclei are the most affected.
  • MERFISH sections are sampled at intervals (tens to a few hundred µm) and only five animals are available; the Zhuang-ABCA-4 brain has only three sections.
  • The ISH volumes are reconstructed from Allen ISH sections ("interp" volumes), so between sections the values are interpolated, and probe quality varies by gene.
  • Only Allen 638850 samples both hemispheres. Zhuang-ABCA-1 and -2 are coronal with about 95% of cells on one side; Zhuang-ABCA-3 and -4 are sagittal with all cells on one side, and -4 covers only LR 1.6–3.1 mm. An empty side is unsampled, not empty of expression.
  • MERFISH colours use each gene's scale within each brain, and the share of cells with signal differs by brain (Gad2: 57% in Allen 638850, 23.5% in Zhuang-ABCA-1). Compare brains with the region tables and concordance, not by colour; signal above zero is not proof of expression.
  • ISH colours are scaled to each gene's own range, so faint genes can look strong. The viewer flags genes with p99.5 ≤ 10 of 255 (1,146 of 4,085). They agree less with MERFISH (median ρ 0.25 against 0.60 for p99.5 > 20, Zhuang-ABCA-1), though some are true sparse markers such as Prox1.
  • MERFISH panels are targeted (500 genes in Allen 638850, 1,122 in Zhuang); a gene missing from a panel is not measured, not absent. The Allen panel also has 50 blank control probes, which are not offered as genes.
  • CCFv3BBP subdivides some regions (e.g. cerebellar cortex and olfactory bulb layers) that CCFv3 does not; cells' own CCF labels cannot reach those subdivisions.
